The Broken Reality

The 45% Early-Career Scam Crisis

  • Disguised Training Fees: Over 40% of posts ask students to pay ₹5K–₹25K for "internship certificates" instead of paying stipends.
  • Ghost & Stale Listings: 15+ hours wasted weekly applying to expired, duplicate, or unmonitored aggregate scraper links.
  • Experience Mislabeling: "Entry-level" and "internship" posts secretly demanding 3+ to 5+ years of production experience.
Result: High applicant burnout & 80% rejection without genuine recruiter contact.
Dual-Layer Moat

Deterministic + LLM Engine

  • Layer 1: Deterministic Filter (0 Cost): Drops unpaid posts, checks India min. stipend (≥ ₹5,000/mo), drops senior & 3+ exp tags, collapses duplicate mass-posters.
  • Layer 2: n8n + LLM Pipeline: Automated deep inspection verifying company genuineness, training detection patterns, and link resolution checks.
  • Round-Robin 5-Batch Moderation: Fast, non-blocking pipeline feeds verified listings sequentially with zero page scrambles.
Efficiency: 70% filtered at ₹0 cost; LLM only deployed for high-yield validation.
